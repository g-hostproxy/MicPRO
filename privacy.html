MicPRO privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

MicPRO is a desktop app that processes your microphone in real time. This policy explains what it does with your information.

What MicPRO accesses

  • Your microphone audio. MicPRO needs it to clean up your voice and apply voice effects. The audio is processed on your computer, in memory, as you speak.
  • Your list of audio devices. MicPRO uses it to let you choose a microphone, an output and headphones, and to remember those choices.

What MicPRO stores

  • Settings such as presets, knob positions, the selected voice, hotkeys and device names. These are saved only on your computer, in MicPRO's app data folder.
  • Recordings, only when you use Record test and choose Save. They're written to the location you pick and nowhere else.

What MicPRO does not do

  • It does not upload, transmit or share your audio, recordings or settings.
  • It has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • It does not sell or disclose any information to third parties, because it collects none.

MicPRO sends processed audio only to the audio device you choose (for example a virtual audio cable). From there it's handled by whatever app you use, such as Discord or OBS, under that app's own privacy policy.

Purchases

If you bought MicPRO from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the payment under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. MicPRO never sees your payment details.

Your controls

Uninstalling MicPRO removes the app and its settings. Recordings you saved stay where you put them until you delete them. You can block MicPRO's microphone access at any time in Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support contact on MicPRO's Microsoft Store page.
