    Get MicPRO into OBS, Discord and your recordings

    1. Install a virtual audio cable. On Windows the free VB-CABLE works well. Restart MicPRO afterwards.
    2. Pick your mic under Devices. For the SM7B, choose your audio interface and the input the mic is plugged into.
    3. Set "Send clean audio to" to CABLE Input.
    4. In OBS, Discord or the game, choose CABLE Output as the microphone. Turn off their own noise suppression and auto gain so audio isn't processed twice.
    5. Load a preset and press Auto-set gain, then talk at your normal raid volume for five seconds.
    6. Record a test and compare raw against processed before you hit record on a raid.

    Logitech Pro X: turn Blue VO!CE off in G HUB and set the mic level to about 80%. Shure SM7B: give it 55–60 dB of clean gain from your interface (or a Cloudlifter / FetHead) before MicPRO adds anything.
